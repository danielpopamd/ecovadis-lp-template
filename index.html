Common questions

What sustainability leaders ask us first

How is EcoVadis different from supplier self-assessments?

Self-assessment questionnaires rely on what suppliers say about themselves. EcoVadis ratings are validated by 500+ sustainability analysts against documentary evidence and external data, so scores hold up with auditors, customers and regulators.

Which regulations does the platform cover?

CSRD, CS3D, LkSG, Modern Slavery Acts, California climate disclosure laws and more. Requirements are mapped to the evidence you already collect through ratings and risk screening.

How long does it take to get started?

Most enterprises see their first supplier insights within weeks. With 150,000+ companies already rated, a large share of your supply base typically has a scorecard on day one.

What if our suppliers don't engage?

Supplier onboarding is handled with you, in 14+ languages, with training and support through the EcoVadis Academy. Participation rates are among the highest in the industry because suppliers reuse one rating across all their customers.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on the size of your supply base and the modules you need. Book a demo and we will scope it with you in the first call.
